【英语】语法详解

发表于 2026-10-30 分类于 英语

这篇按高中英语的学习顺序来写：先看句子是怎么搭的，再看词、时态、语态，最后看从句和几个特殊句式。每个语法点都按同一套方法讲。

  1. 先看公式，知道词放在什么位置。
  2. 再看肯定、否定、疑问，自己能把一句话变出三种形式。
  3. 再看例句，每句后面都有中文。
  4. 最后看“别写成”，这是高中生最容易丢分的地方。

读例句时，先找出谁是主语、哪个词是谓语，再看时间是现在、过去还是将来。抓不住主语和谓语，后面的从句、时态都会混。

一、先会拆句子

英语句子再长，骨架也只有主语和谓语。其他词都是往这副骨架上加的。

拿下面这句话做例子：

Yesterday my friend Tom quietly gave me a useful book in the library.

词 成分 为什么
Yesterday 时间状语 说明什么时候
my friend 主语 动作是谁发出的
Tom 同位语 再解释 my friend 是谁
quietly 方式状语 说明怎么给
gave 谓语 句子的动词
me 间接宾语 给谁
a useful book 直接宾语 给的东西；useful 是定语，修饰 book
in the library 地点状语 在哪里

1. 八种成分，各自干什么

主语：句子说的是谁或什么。名词、代词、动名词、不定式、从句都可以当主语。

  • The cat is sleeping. 猫在睡觉。
  • Swimming is good exercise. 游泳是很好的锻炼。
  • To learn English takes time. 学英语需要时间。

谓语：说明主语做什么、是什么。一个简单句只能有一个谓语动词（并列谓语除外）。

  • She reads every night. 她每晚读书。
  • She reads and writes every night. 她每晚又读又写。（两个谓语并列）

宾语：动作的对象。及物动词后面才有宾语。

  • I bought a book. 我买了一本书。
  • I enjoy reading. 我喜欢阅读。

表语：跟在系动词后面，说明主语“是什么样”。

  • The soup is hot. 汤是热的。
  • He became a doctor. 他成了一名医生。

常见系动词要单独记，它们后面不是宾语，是表语：be、become、get、turn、grow、seem、appear、look、feel、sound、taste、smell、remain、stay、keep。

  • The music sounds beautiful. 这音乐听起来很美。
  • 别写成 sounds beautifully。sound 在这里是系动词，后面用形容词。

定语：修饰名词，翻译成“……的”。

  • a red car 一辆红色的车
  • the girl in red 穿红衣服的女孩
  • the book on the desk 桌上的书

状语：修饰动词、形容词、副词或整句，回答何时、何地、为何、如何、到什么程度。

  • He left quietly. 他悄悄离开了。
  • He left at six. 他六点离开了。
  • He left because he was tired. 他因为累了才离开。

补语：把宾语或主语的意思补完整。去掉它，句子意思会缺一块。

  • We call him Tom. 我们叫他汤姆。只说 We call him，听的人不知道叫他什么。
  • Please keep the door closed. 请让门保持关着。

同位语：跟在名词后面，和它指同一个人或事物。

  • My friend Anna lives here. 我的朋友安娜住在这里。
  • Mr Li, our English teacher, is kind. 李老师，也就是我们的英语老师，人很好。

2. 五种基本句型

先判断动词属于哪一种，句型就定了。

怎么判断：

  1. 动词后面能不能直接加“某物”？不能，多半是句型 1 或句型 3。
  2. 动词是 be、become、look 这类，说明主语“是什么、怎么样”，就是句型 3。
  3. 动词后面有两个东西，一个是人、一个是物，就是句型 4。
  4. 动词后面先有一个宾语，还要补上“成为什么、处于什么状态”，就是句型 5。

句型 1：主语 + 不及物动词

动词本身意思完整，后面不接宾语。可以加状语。

  • Birds fly. 鸟会飞。
  • The baby cried. 婴儿哭了。
  • The sun rises in the east. 太阳从东方升起。

句型 2：主语 + 及物动词 + 宾语

  • She opened the door. 她打开了门。
  • They speak English. 他们说英语。
  • I like music. 我喜欢音乐。

句型 3：主语 + 系动词 + 表语

  • He is a teacher. 他是老师。
  • The leaves turn yellow. 叶子变黄了。
  • The plan seems possible. 这个计划似乎可行。

句型 4：主语 + 动词 + 间接宾语 + 直接宾语

间接宾语多是人，直接宾语多是物。

  • She gave me a gift. 她给了我一份礼物。
  • 也可以把人放到后面，用介词：She gave a gift to me.

什么时候用 to，什么时候用 for：

  • 表示“给某人”，用 to：give、send、show、tell、lend、pass、teach、bring。
    • Please show the photo to me. 请把照片给我看。
  • 表示“为某人做”，用 for：buy、make、cook、get、find、sing。
    • He bought a bike for his son. 他给儿子买了一辆自行车。

句型 5：主语 + 动词 + 宾语 + 宾语补足语

补语说明宾语变成什么样、处在什么状态。

  • We elected her captain. 我们选她当队长。
  • I found the film interesting. 我觉得这部电影有意思。
  • Please keep the door closed. 请让门关着。
  • The teacher made us laugh. 老师把我们逗笑了。

句型 4 和句型 5 容易混。用这个办法：把两个名词对调，意思还在，就是双宾语；对调之后不成句，就是宾补。

  • She gave me a gift. 可以换成 She gave a gift to me. 这是双宾语。
  • We elected her captain. 不能说 We elected captain to her. 这是宾补。captain 是在说明 her 的身份。

3. 四种句子，按用途分

陈述句就是告诉别人一件事。

  • She works in Beijing. 她在北京工作。
  • She does not work in Beijing. 她不在北京工作。

疑问句用来提问，下一节“特殊句式”里会把一般疑问、特殊疑问、选择疑问、反意疑问分开讲。这里先看最基本的变化。

有 be 动词时，把 be 提前：

  • You are ready. → Are you ready? 你准备好了吗？

有情态动词时，把情态动词提前：

  • She can drive. → Can she drive? 她会开车吗？

只有实义动词时，借助 do / does / did，后面动词用原形：

  • You like tea. → Do you like tea? 你喜欢茶吗？
  • He likes tea. → Does he like tea? 他喜欢茶吗？
  • 别写成 Does he likes tea? does 已经表示第三人称，likes 不能再加 s。

祈使句用来下命令、提要求、给劝告。主语 you 通常不说出来。

  • Open the window. 把窗户打开。
  • Be quiet. 安静。
  • Don’t be late. 别迟到。
  • Let’s go. 我们走吧。（包括说话人自己）
  • Let us go. 让我们走吧。（请求对方允许）
  • Never give up. 永远不要放弃。

感叹句表达强烈感情。先看感叹的中心是名词还是形容词、副词。

中心是名词，用 What：

  • What + a/an + 形容词 + 可数名词单数 + (主语 + 谓语)!
    • What a nice day (it is)! 多好的天气！
    • What an interesting book (it is)! 多有意思的书！
  • 名词不可数或复数时，不加 a/an：
    • What fine weather! 多好的天气！
    • What beautiful flowers! 多美的花！

中心是形容词或副词，用 How：

  • How + 形容词/副词 + 主语 + 谓语!
    • How beautiful the lake is! 湖真美！
    • How carefully she writes! 她写得多仔细！
    • How time flies! 时间过得真快！

同一个意思可以换架子：What a clever boy he is! = How clever the boy is!

二、名词

名词给人、事物、地点、概念起名字。高中阶段主要考四件事：可数还是不可数、复数怎么变、所有格、名词能不能直接修饰另一个名词。

1. 可数名词和不可数名词

可数名词能数出个数，有单数和复数，单数前可以加 a 或 an。

  • a book 一本书
  • two books 两本书

不可数名词表示一整类物质、抽象概念，一般没有复数，也不能直接加 a/an。要表示“一份、一条”，就加量词。

  • water 水 → a glass of water 一杯水
  • advice 建议 → a piece of advice 一条建议
  • information 信息 → two pieces of information 两条信息
  • furniture 家具 → a piece of furniture 一件家具
  • news 消息 → a piece of news 一则消息

高中常考、不要加 s 的不可数名词：advice、information、news、furniture、homework、knowledge、progress、weather、fun、luggage、baggage、equipment、traffic。

  • The news is good. 消息是好的。news 看起来像复数，其实是不可数名词，动词用 is。
  • 别写成 advices、informations。

有些词可数和不可数意思不同，必须看句子：

  • paper 纸（不可数）；a paper 一份报纸、一篇论文

  • room 空间（不可数）；a room 一个房间

  • experience 经验（不可数）；an experience 一次经历

  • time 时间（不可数）；three times 三次

  • work 工作（不可数）；works 作品、工厂

  • chicken 鸡肉（不可数）；a chicken 一只鸡

  • glass 玻璃（不可数）；a glass 一只玻璃杯

  • There is no room for the desk. 没有地方放这张桌子。

  • There are three rooms upstairs. 楼上有三个房间。

2. 复数怎么变

按结尾字母走，不要全部加 s。

  • 大多数直接加 -s：book → books，car → cars，boy → boys
  • 以 s、x、ch、sh 结尾，加 -es：bus → buses，box → boxes，watch → watches，dish → dishes
  • 辅音字母 + y，把 y 改成 i，再加 -es：city → cities，baby → babies
  • 元音字母 + y，直接加 -s：boy → boys，key → keys，day → days
  • 以 f 或 fe 结尾，多数把 f/fe 改成 ves：leaf → leaves，knife → knives，wife → wives，life → lives，half → halves
  • 下面几个只加 -s：roof → roofs，belief → beliefs，chief → chiefs，proof → proofs
  • 以 o 结尾，有生命或常见食物多加 -es：tomato → tomatoes，potato → potatoes，hero → heroes
  • 外来词、乐器、缩写多加 -s：photo → photos，piano → pianos，radio → radios，kilo → kilos

不规则复数要整词记：

  • man → men，woman → women，child → children
  • foot → feet，tooth → teeth，goose → geese，mouse → mice
  • sheep → sheep，deer → deer，fish → fish（表示不同种类时可以用 fishes）
  • Chinese → Chinese，Japanese → Japanese
  • phenomenon → phenomena，criterion → criteria

复合名词的复数通常加在中心词上：

  • a passer-by → passers-by 过路人
  • a son-in-law → sons-in-law 女婿
  • a grown-up → grown-ups 成年人

3. 所有格：谁的

有生命的名词加 ‘s。

  • Tom’s book 汤姆的书
  • the children’s toys 孩子们的玩具（children 本身不是以 s 结尾，所以加 ‘s）
  • the teachers’ office 教师办公室（teachers 已经以 s 结尾，只加撇号）

两个人共有一样东西，’s 加在最后一个人后面；各自拥有，每个人都加。

  • Tom and Jerry’s room 汤姆和杰瑞合住的那一间
  • Tom’s and Jerry’s rooms 汤姆的房间和杰瑞的房间（两间）

无生命的东西多用 of。

  • the door of the room 房间的门
  • the end of the story 故事的结尾

时间、距离、国家、机构、城市可以用 ‘s。

  • today’s news 今天的新闻
  • ten minutes’ walk 步行十分钟的路程
  • China’s capital 中国的首都

在店铺、住宅、诊所后面，表示地点的名词可以省掉。

  • at the doctor’s 在诊所
  • at my uncle’s 在我叔叔家

4. 名词修饰名词

名词当定语时，一般用单数，表示类别，不表示数量。

  • a shoe shop 鞋店
  • two apple trees 两棵苹果树
  • a bus driver 公交车司机
  • a woman teacher 一位女老师；两位是 two women teachers（woman 要一起变复数）

少数习惯用复数：a sports meeting 运动会，a clothes shop 服装店，a goods train 货车。

三、冠词

冠词只有三个选择：a/an、the、不加。先问两个问题。

  1. 这个名词是可数单数，而且是第一次提到、不具体指哪一个吗？用 a 或 an。
  2. 说话双方都知道是哪一个，或者世界上只有一个吗？用 the。
  3. 是三餐、球类、学科、语言，或者泛指一类物质吗？常常不加。

1. a 和 an

a 和 an 都是“一个”，只用于可数名词单数。看后面单词的发音，不看字母。

  • a book，a university（university 开头是 /j/，辅音）
  • an apple，an hour（hour 的 h 不发音），an honest man
  • an MBA student（M 读作 /em/，元音开头）
  • a one-way street（one 开头是 /w/）

用法：

  • 第一次提到某个人或物：I saw a dog. The dog was hungry. 我看见一只狗。那只狗饿了。第二次已经知道是哪一只，改用 the。
  • 职业：She is a doctor. 她是医生。
  • 频率、价格、速度：three times a week 一周三次，60 kilometers an hour 每小时 60 公里。

2. the

  • 双方都知道：Close the door. 把门关上。
  • 独一无二：the sun，the moon，the earth，the world
  • 序数词、最高级前面：the first lesson 第一课，the best student 最好的学生
  • the + 形容词，表示一类人：the rich 富人，the poor 穷人，the young 年轻人。动词用复数：The rich are not always happy.
  • 西洋乐器：play the piano，play the violin。球类和棋类不加：play basketball，play chess。
  • 江河湖海、山脉、海峡：the Yangtze River，the Pacific Ocean，the Himalayas
  • 普通名词构成的专有名词：the Great Wall，the United States，the Summer Palace
  • 姓氏复数表示一家人：the Greens 格林一家
  • in the morning，in the afternoon，in the evening。但 at night、at noon 不加 the。

3. 不加冠词

  • 三餐：have breakfast / lunch / supper。如果餐名前有形容词，就要加 a：have a big breakfast。
  • 球类、学科、语言：play football，study physics，speak Chinese
  • 职务作表语、补语、同位语：He was elected president. 他当选为总统。
  • 星期、月份、季节泛指：on Monday，in July，in spring。特指某一个春天才加 the：in the spring of 2020。
  • by + 交通方式：by bus，by train，by air。换成具体工具就要加冠词：on a bus，in his car。
  • 成对的词：day and night，husband and wife，face to face

几组必须对比着记：

  • go to school 去上学；go to the school 到那所学校去（办事，不一定上学）
  • go to bed 去睡觉；go to the bed 走到那张床边
  • in hospital（英式）住院；in the hospital 在医院这座楼里
  • in front of the car 在汽车前面（车外）；in the front of the car 在汽车前排（车内）
  • at table 在吃饭；at the table 在桌子旁

四、代词

代词用来代替前面出现过的名词。用错格，是因为没有先看它在句子里当主语还是当宾语。

1. 人称代词

主格（作主语） 宾格（作宾语）
我 I me
你 you you
他 / 她 / 它 he / she / it him / her / it
我们 we us
你们 you you
他们 they them
  • I am ready. 我准备好了。
  • Please help me. 请帮我。
  • Between you and me, he is wrong. 私下跟你说，他错了。between 是介词，后面用宾格。
  • 强调句里仍用主格：It is she who called. 打电话的人是她。口语里说 It’s me 也可以。

几个人称并列时，单数顺序通常是 you, he and I；复数是 we, you and they。承认错误时把 I 放前面：I and he are to blame.

2. 物主代词

形容词性物主代词后面必须再接名词：my、your、his、her、its、our、their。

  • This is my seat. 这是我的座位。
  • The dog wagged its tail. 狗摇了摇尾巴。its 表示“它的”，没有撇号。it’s = it is。

名词性物主代词等于“形容词性物主代词 + 名词”，后面不再接名词：mine、yours、his、hers、ours、theirs。

  • This seat is mine. = This seat is my seat.
  • Your bag is heavier than mine. 你的包比我的重。
  • 别写成 than my。than 后面如果省略了 bag，要用名词性物主代词。

3. 反身代词

myself、yourself、himself、herself、itself、ourselves、yourselves、themselves。

主语和宾语是同一个人，用反身代词。

  • She taught herself French. 她自学法语。
  • Help yourself to some fruit. 请随便吃水果。
  • The door opened by itself. 门自己开了。
  • I myself checked the list. 我亲自核对了名单。

enjoy、help、teach、dress、devote 后面常接反身代词：enjoy oneself 玩得开心，dress oneself 自己穿衣服。

  • We enjoyed ourselves at the party. 我们在晚会上玩得很开心。

4. 指示代词

  • this / these 指近处，也可指下文要说的事。
    • This is my plan. 这就是我的计划。
  • that / those 指远处，也可指上文刚说过的事。
    • That was a hard year. 那是艰难的一年。
  • 打电话：This is Tom speaking. Who is that? 我是汤姆。你是哪位？
  • 比较时，为了不重复名词，单数用 that，复数用 those。
    • The weather here is warmer than that in Beijing. 这里的天气比北京的天气暖和。that 代替 weather。
    • The books on this shelf are newer than those on that one. those 代替 books。
    • 别写成 than Beijing。比的是天气，不是城市。

it、one、that 都用来代替前面的名词，分工不同：

  • it 指同一个事物：I bought a pen, but I lost it. 我买了一支笔，又弄丢了。（就是那一支）
  • one 指同一类里的另一个：I don’t like this pen. Please show me another one.
  • that 多用于比较结构，代替不可数名词或有后置定语的单数名词，见上面的 weather。

5. 不定代词

some 和 any

  • some 用在肯定句，也用在希望得到肯定回答的问句里。
    • I have some questions. 我有一些问题。
    • Would you like some tea? 喝点茶吗？（希望对方要）
  • any 用在否定句和疑问句，也表示“任何一个”。
    • I don’t have any questions. 我没有问题。
    • You may take any book you like. 你喜欢哪本就拿哪本。

many 和 much，few 和 little

可数 不可数
许多 many / a lot of much / a lot of
有一些（肯定） a few a little
几乎没有（否定） few little
  • I have a few friends here. 我在这儿有几个朋友。
  • Few people agreed. 几乎没有人同意。
  • There is a little milk left. 还剩一点牛奶。
  • There is little milk left. 牛奶差不多没了。
  • only a few、only a little 仍然表示少：Only a few students passed. 只有几个学生通过了。

both、either、neither；all、none

  • both 两者都：Both answers are correct. 两个答案都对。
  • either 两者中任意一个：You may take either road. 两条路走哪条都行。Either of the answers is OK.
  • neither 两者都不：Neither of them came. 他们俩都没来。Neither answer is right.
  • all 三者及以上都：All of the seats are taken.
  • none 三者及以上都不：None of the answers is / are right. 这些答案没有一个对。
  • both、all 和 not 在一起，是部分否定。
    • Not all the students are here. = All the students are not here. 并非所有学生都在。
    • 要说“所有人都不”，用 none：None of the students are here.

each 和 every

  • each 强调一个一个，可以作主语：Each student has a locker. Each of them has a locker.
  • every 强调整体，后面必须接名词，不能说 every of them：Every student has a locker.

another、other、the other、others

  • another + 单数：再来一个，范围不确定。Give me another cup. 再给我一杯。
  • one … the other …：两个里面的另一个。I have two pens. One is blue, and the other is black.
  • some … others …：一些……另一些……（没有范围）。Some students are reading; others are writing.
  • some … the others …：一些……其余所有人……。Some are reading; the others are writing.
  • another + 数词 + 复数名词：另外几个。I need another three days. 我还需要三天。也可以说 three more days。

复合不定代词

something、anything、nothing、everything、somebody、anybody、nobody、everybody。

  • 形容词放在它们后面：something important，nothing special，Is there anything new?
  • 作主语时，动词用单数：Everybody is here. Nobody knows the answer.
  • 否定句里通常用 anything，不用 something：I don’t know anything about it.

6. 疑问代词

  • Who called you? 谁给你打的电话？（who 作主语，语序不变）
  • Whom did you meet? 你遇见了谁？（whom 作宾语；口语常用 who）
  • Whose bag is this? 这是谁的包？
  • What happened? 发生了什么事？
  • What is your father? 你父亲是做什么的？（问职业）
  • Who is that man? 那人是谁？（问身份）
  • Which color do you prefer, red or blue? 红和蓝你更喜欢哪个？（在已知范围里选）
  • What subject do you like? 你喜欢什么科目？（没有给出范围）

五、数词

1. 基数词和序数词

基数词表示数量，序数词表示顺序。

  • one → first，two → second，three → third
  • four → fourth，five → fifth，eight → eighth，nine → ninth，twelve → twelfth
  • twenty → twentieth，thirty → thirtieth
  • twenty-one → twenty-first，twenty-two → twenty-second

几十几的序数词，只变最后一部分：twenty-one 的序数是 twenty-first，不是 twenty-oneth。

日期、世纪、楼层用序数词：

  • May 1st，读作 May the first
  • the 21st century 21 世纪
  • on the third floor 在三楼

编号有两种说法：

  • Lesson Five = the fifth lesson 第五课
  • Room 302 读作 Room three oh two
  • Page 10 读作 Page ten

hundred、thousand、million 前面有具体数字时不加 s：three hundred students。表示不确切的大量才加 s，并跟 of：hundreds of students 数百名学生。

2. 分数、小数、百分数

分数：分子用基数词，分母用序数词。分子大于 1，分母加 s。

  • 1/3 one third
  • 2/3 two thirds
  • 1/2 a half，也可以说 one second，但 a half 更常见
  • 1/4 a quarter
  • 3/4 three quarters

分数、百分数作主语时，动词看后面的名词。

  • Two thirds of the water is polluted. 三分之二的水被污染了。
  • Two thirds of the students are absent. 三分之二的学生缺席。
  • Thirty percent of the land is covered by forest.

小数点读 point：3.14 读作 three point one four。0.5 读作 zero point five。

3. 倍数

高中写作优先用下面两种，意思清楚。

  • A is twice as big as B. A 是 B 的两倍大。（A = 2 × B）
  • A is three times the size of B. A 的大小是 B 的三倍。
  • 长度、宽度、数量可以换成 the length / the width / the number of。
    • This rope is three times the length of that one. 这根绳子的长度是那根的三倍。

“A is twice bigger than B”容易让人理解成 A = B + 2B，也有人理解成两倍。考试写作时改用 as … as 或 times the size of。

年龄、重量也可以这样说：

  • He is twice as old as I am. 他的年龄是我的两倍。
  • This box is three times as heavy as that one.

4. 时间的读法

  • 8:00 eight o’clock
  • 8:15 eight fifteen，或 a quarter past eight
  • 8:30 eight thirty，或 half past eight
  • 8:45 eight forty-five，或 a quarter to nine
  • 8:50 ten to nine

六、形容词和副词

形容词修饰名词，副词修饰动词、形容词、副词或整句。很多副词由“形容词 + ly”构成，但不是全部。

  • careful → carefully，slow → slowly
  • 本身既是形容词又是副词：fast、hard、late、early、straight
    • a fast train；He runs fast.
  • hard 努力地、猛烈地；hardly 几乎不。两者意思差很远。
    • He works hard. 他工作努力。
    • He hardly works. 他几乎不工作。

1. 形容词放在哪里

  • 一般在名词前：a quiet street
  • 在 something、anything、nothing 后面：nothing special，something important
  • 在系动词后面作表语：The milk smells sour. The soup tastes good.
    • 别写成 tastes well。taste 是系动词时，后面用形容词 good，不用副词 well。well 作形容词时只表示“身体好”：I don’t feel well.

有些形容词只能作表语，不能放在名词前：afraid、alone、asleep、awake、alive、alike、ashamed、aware。

  • The baby is asleep. 婴儿睡着了。
  • 名词前要换一个词：a sleeping baby，不能说 an asleep baby。
  • 人还活着：He is still alive. 不能说 an alive man，要说 a living man。

多个形容词叠在一起，考试很少考超长的一串，记住这个顺序就够：

观点 → 大小 → 年龄或新旧 → 形状 → 颜色 → 产地 → 材料 → 用途

  • a beautiful small old round table 一张漂亮的旧小圆桌
  • a Chinese wooden writing desk 一张中式木制写字桌

口诀可以记“美小圆旧黄，法国木书房”，对应观点、大小、形状、新旧、颜色、产地、材料、用途。

2. 副词放在哪里

  • 方式副词多在动词后面：She spoke slowly. 她说得很慢。
  • 程度副词在形容词或副词前面：too heavy，very carefully，almost finished
  • 频率副词在实义动词前面、be 动词后面、第一个助动词后面。
    • He is always late.
    • He always arrives late.
    • He has never been there. 他从没去过那里。
  • 地点状语在前，时间状语在后：I’ll meet you at the station at six. 我六点在车站见你。
  • 一句里如果方式、地点、时间都有，常见顺序是方式 + 地点 + 时间：She sang beautifully at the concert last night.

enough 的位置取决于它修饰谁。

  • 修饰形容词、副词，放后面：big enough，fast enough
    • The room is big enough for us. 房间对我们来说够大。
    • He didn’t run fast enough. 他跑得不够快。
  • 修饰名词，放前面：enough time，enough books
    • We have enough time. 我们时间够。

too … to … 表示“太……而不能……”。

  • The box is too heavy to carry. 箱子太重，搬不动。
  • He is too young to go to school. 他太小，还不能上学。
  • 可以改成 so … that … not：The box is so heavy that I can’t carry it.

so 和 such 都表示“如此”，后面的词性不同。

  • so + 形容词/副词：so heavy，so fast
  • such + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词：such a heavy box，such fine weather，such beautiful flowers
  • so + many / much / few / little + 名词：so many books，so little time
    • 这里的 little 是“少”，不是“小”。小用 such a little boy。

3. 比较级和最高级

怎么构成

一个音节，以及以 y、er、ow、le 结尾的双音节词，加 -er、-est。

  • tall → taller → tallest
  • late → later → latest
  • big → bigger → biggest（重读闭音节，双写最后一个辅音）
  • hot → hotter → hottest
  • happy → happier → happiest（y 变 i）
  • thin → thinner → thinnest

两个音节及以上，多加 more、most。

  • beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful
  • careful → more careful → most careful
  • slowly → more slowly → most slowly

不规则的要整组记：

  • good / well → better → best
  • bad / badly → worse → worst
  • many / much → more → most
  • little → less → least
  • far → farther / further → farthest / furthest
    • farther 多指距离：The station is farther than the school.
    • further 还可以指“进一步”：further information 进一步的信息
  • old → older / elder → oldest / eldest
    • older 可用于人或物；elder 只用于家人兄弟姐妹的长幼，后面常接名词：my elder brother 我哥哥

四种句型

两者相比，用比较级。

  • This road is wider than that one. 这条路比那条宽。
  • 比较级前面可以加 much、a lot、far、even、a little、a bit，表示差多少。
    • This bag is much heavier than that one. 这个包重得多。
    • He is a little taller than I am. 他比我高一点。
  • 不能加 very、more 来修饰已经是比较级的词。别写成 more taller、very taller。

三者及以上，用最高级，前面加 the，并说出范围。

  • She is the tallest in the class. 她是班里最高的。
  • This is the most interesting book of the three. 这是三本里最有意思的。
  • 副词最高级前面的 the 可以省略：She runs (the) fastest in our team.

一样程度，用 as + 原级 + as。

  • He is as tall as his brother. 他和他哥哥一样高。
  • It is not as cold as yesterday. = It is not so cold as yesterday. 今天没有昨天冷。
  • 否定也可以改成比较级：It is less cold than yesterday. 或者 Today is warmer than yesterday.

越来越，用比较级 + and + 比较级。多音节词用 more and more + 原级。

  • The city is getting bigger and bigger. 这座城市越来越大。
  • It is becoming more and more expensive. 它越来越贵。

越……就越……，用 the + 比较级，the + 比较级。

  • The harder you work, the better result you will get. 你越努力，结果就越好。
  • The more you read, the more you know.

比较时两边要对等。

  • The weather in Kunming is better than that in Harbin. 昆明的天气比哈尔滨的天气好。that 代替 weather。
  • 别写成 than Harbin。那样就成了“天气”和“哈尔滨这座城市”比较。

七、动词

1. 及物和不及物

及物动词后面必须有宾语，不及物动词不能直接带宾语。不确定时，先看中文里这个动作有没有“对象”。

  • 及物：She raised her hand. 她举起了手。raise 的对象是 hand。
  • 不及物：The sun rises. 太阳升起。太阳自己升，没有对象。
  • 同一个词两种用法，意思不同：
    • He runs every morning. 他每天早上跑步。
    • She runs a small shop. 她经营一家小店。

高中最容易混的几对：

词 过去式 过去分词 及物吗 例句
lie 躺 lay lain 不及物 Please lie down. He lay on the bed.
lie 说谎 lied lied 不及物 He lied to me.
lay 放置 laid laid 及物 He laid the book on the desk.
rise 升起 rose risen 不及物 Prices rose.
raise 举起、提高 raised raised 及物 They raised the price.
sit 坐 sat sat 不及物 Please sit down.
seat 使就座 seated seated 及物 He seated the guests. He was seated by the window.

2. 短语动词

动词加一个小品词后，意思常常不能按字面猜，要整块记。

  • look after 照顾：She looks after her grandmother.
  • look for 寻找：I’m looking for my keys.
  • look up 查阅：Look the word up in a dictionary.
  • look forward to 期待：I look forward to seeing you. 这里的 to 是介词，后面用 doing。
  • give up 放弃：Don’t give up.
  • put off 推迟：They put off the meeting.
  • put on 穿上：Put on your coat.
  • take off 脱下；起飞：Take off your shoes. The plane took off.
  • turn down 拒绝；把声音调低：He turned down the offer. Turn down the radio.
  • turn up 出现；调高：He didn’t turn up. Turn up the radio.
  • pick up 捡起；开车接：Pick up the pen. I’ll pick you up at seven.
  • get along with 与……相处：She gets along well with her classmates.

小品词如果是副词，代词宾语必须放在中间。

  • Turn it off. 把它关掉。
  • 别写成 Turn off it.
  • 名词宾语两边都可以：Turn off the light. = Turn the light off.

小品词如果是介词，宾语无论名词还是代词都放在后面。

  • Look after her. 照顾她。
  • 不能说 Look her after.

怎么区分：after、for、with、at、into 多是介词；off、up、down、away、out 在这些短语里常常是副词。拿不准时，用代词试一下位置。

八、时态

时态回答两个问题：事情发生在什么时间，是正在进行、已经完成，还是平时如此。

高中真正天天用的是前八个。后面八个先会认、会在宾语从句里用，不必一开始就平均用力。

一般 进行 完成 完成进行
现在 do / does am/is/are doing have/has done have/has been doing
过去 did was/were doing had done had been doing
将来 will do will be doing will have done will have been doing
过去将来 would do would be doing would have done would have been doing

变否定和疑问时记住一条：句子里已经有 be、助动词或情态动词，就用它来否定、提前；没有，才请 do / does / did 来帮忙。请了 do，后面的动词必须回到原形。

1. 一般现在时

什么时候用

  • 习惯、反复发生的事：She walks to school. 她步行上学。
  • 客观事实、真理：Water boils at 100°C. 水在 100 摄氏度沸腾。
  • 时刻表、日程：The train leaves at 7:30. 火车 7:30 开。
  • 时间状语从句和条件状语从句里，用现在时表示将来：If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.

以 work 为例，自己变三种形式

主语是 I / you / we / they：

  • 肯定：I work every day.
  • 否定：I do not work every day. 缩写 don’t。
  • 疑问：Do you work every day?

主语是 he / she / it：

  • 肯定：He works every day. 动词加 s。
  • 否定：He does not work every day. works 还原成 work。
  • 疑问：Does he work every day?

加 s 的规则和名词复数差不多：watch → watches，study → studies，go → goes，have → has。

标志词：always、usually、often、sometimes、seldom、never、every day、on Sundays、once a week。

别写成

  • He don’t like English. 第三人称要用 doesn’t。
  • Does he likes English? does 和 likes 只能留一个 s，应是 Does he like English?

2. 现在进行时

公式：am / is / are + 动词-ing

什么时候用

  • 说话这一刻正在做：I am reading. 我正在读。
  • 现阶段暂时的情况，此刻不一定正在做：She is staying with her aunt this week. 这周她暂住姑姑家。
  • 已经安排好的将来：We are flying to Shanghai tomorrow. 我们明天飞上海。

三种形式

  • I am reading.
  • I am not reading.
  • Are you reading?

动词-ing 怎么写

  • 一般直接加 ing：work → working，read → reading
  • 不发音的 e 结尾，去掉 e：make → making，write → writing。see → seeing（ee 要保留）
  • 重读闭音节、末尾只有一个辅音，双写再加 ing：run → running，sit → sitting，swim → swimming
  • ie 结尾，把 ie 改成 y：lie → lying，die → dying

一般不用于进行时的状态动词：know、believe、understand、love、like、hate、want、need、own、belong、seem。这些词表示状态，不是动作。

  • I know the answer. 别写成 I am knowing the answer.

和一般现在时怎么分

  • She works in a bank. 她在银行工作。（长期职业）
  • She is working in a bank this month. 她这个月在银行干活。（暂时）

3. 现在完成时

公式：have / has + 过去分词

什么时候用

现在完成时把过去和现在连在一起。常见三种情况。

  • 经历：到现在为止做过没有。I have visited Xi’an twice. 我去过西安两次。
  • 结果：过去的动作影响到现在。She has lost her keys. 她把钥匙弄丢了，所以现在进不了门。
  • 持续：从过去一直延续到现在。He has lived here since 2018. 他从 2018 年住到现在。

三种形式

  • I have finished.
  • I have not finished. 缩写 haven’t。
  • Have you finished?
  • He has finished. / He hasn’t finished. / Has he finished?

标志词

  • already 已经，多用于肯定句：I have already finished.
  • yet 还，用于否定和疑问：I haven’t finished yet. Have you finished yet?
  • ever 曾经，never 从未：Have you ever been to Beijing? I have never been there.
  • just 刚刚：She has just left.
  • since + 时间点，for + 时间段：since 2018，since Monday，for eight years，for two hours
  • so far、up to now、in the past few years、recently、lately

和一般过去时怎么分

关键看时间有没有“说死在过去”。

  • I saw him yesterday. 我昨天看见他了。yesterday 是明确的过去时间，用过去时。
  • I have seen him. 我见过他。没说哪一天，强调经历。
  • 别把明确的过去时间和完成时放在一起：I have seen him yesterday 是错的。要么去掉 yesterday，要么改成 I saw him yesterday。

have been to、have gone to、have been in 三个都要会：

  • He has been to Shanghai. 他去过上海。（人已经回来了）
  • He has gone to Shanghai. 他去上海了。（人还在路上或已经在上海，不在说话现场）
  • He has been in Shanghai for a week. 他在上海已经待了一周。（人还在那里）

4. 现在完成进行时

公式：have / has been + 动词-ing

强调从过去一直做到现在，动作可能还没停，侧重过程，不侧重结果。

  • I have been waiting for an hour. 我已经等了一个小时了。（还在等，而且等得很久）
  • It has been raining all morning. 整个上午都在下雨。
  • 否定：I have not been waiting.
  • 疑问：Have you been waiting long?

和现在完成时对比：

  • I have read the book. 我把书读完了。（有结果）
  • I have been reading the book. 我一直在读这本书。（过程，未必读完）

状态动词、以及 live、work、study、teach 这类可以表示持续的动词，两种时态常常都可以：He has lived here for ten years. = He has been living here for ten years.

5. 一般过去时

什么时候用：过去某个时间发生并且结束了。

  • We moved here in 2019. 我们 2019 年搬到了这里。
  • 肯定：I finished the work.
  • 否定：I did not finish the work. finish 用原形。
  • 疑问：Did you finish the work?

规则动词加 -ed：work → worked，live → lived，stop → stopped，study → studied。不规则动词用上一节的表。

标志词：yesterday、last night、last week、in 2019、ago、just now、the other day、when I was a child。

6. 过去进行时

公式：was / were + 动词-ing

表示过去某一时刻正在做，也常常给另一个过去的短动作当背景。

  • At 8 last night, they were watching a film. 昨晚八点他们正在看电影。
  • I was cooking when the phone rang. 电话响的时候，我正在做饭。
    • was cooking 是延续的背景，rang 是突然插进来的短动作。
  • 否定：I was not cooking.
  • 疑问：Were you cooking?

when 和 while 可以这样记：

  • when 后面可以接短动作：when the phone rang
  • while 后面多接延续的动作：while I was cooking
  • While I was cooking, the phone rang. = I was cooking when the phone rang.

7. 过去完成时

公式：had + 过去分词

表示“过去的过去”。句子里通常有两个过去的时间，先发生的用过去完成时。

时间线：电影开始 → 我到达 → 现在说话

  • When I arrived, the film had already started. 我到的时候，电影已经开始了。
  • She said she had finished the work. 她说她已经做完了。said 是过去，做完在 said 之前。
  • 否定：The film had not started.
  • 疑问：Had the film started?

by the time、before、after、when 常用来把两个过去动作排顺序。

  • By the time we got there, the bus had left. 我们到那里时，公交车已经走了。

8. 过去完成进行时

公式：had been + 动词-ing

在过去某一时刻之前，一个动作已经持续了一段时间。

  • He had been working for ten hours before he stopped. 他停下来之前，已经连续工作了十个小时。
  • I had been waiting for twenty minutes when she arrived. 她到的时候，我已经等了二十分钟。

9. 一般将来时

将来时不止 will 一种。先看说话人是临时决定、早就打算，还是按时刻表发生。

will / shall + 动词原形

  • 预测：It will rain tonight. 今晚会下雨。
  • 临时决定：The phone is ringing. I’ll answer it. 电话响了，我去接。（说话这一刻才决定）
  • 承诺、意愿：I will help you. 我来帮你。
  • 否定：I will not go. 缩写 won’t。
  • 疑问：Will you go?

shall 用于 I / we，征求意见：Shall we begin? 我们开始好吗？

be going to + 动词原形

  • 早就有的打算：I’m going to learn Spanish. 我打算学西班牙语。
  • 有迹象表明即将发生：Look at the clouds. It is going to rain. 看那些云，要下雨了。

现在进行时表将来：已经安排好的个人计划。We are meeting at six. 我们约在六点见面。

一般现在时表将来：时刻表。The plane takes off at nine. 飞机九点起飞。

be about to 表示马上就要，通常不和具体时间状语连用。

  • The train is about to leave. 火车马上要开了。
  • 别写成 The train is about to leave at nine. 有具体钟点就改用现在进行或一般现在。

10. 将来进行时

公式：will be + 动词-ing

将来某个时刻正在做，或者按计划将会发生。语气有时比 will do 更委婉。

  • This time tomorrow I will be flying to Chengdu. 明天这个时候，我将在飞往成都的飞机上。
  • Will you be using the car tonight? 你今晚用车吗？

11. 将来完成时

公式：will have + 过去分词

到将来某个时间为止，动作已经完成。标志是 by + 将来时间。

  • By next June she will have graduated. 到明年六月，她就毕业了。
  • I will have finished the book by Friday. 到星期五，我就把这本书读完了。

12. 将来完成进行时

公式：will have been + 动词-ing

到将来某个时间，动作已经持续了多久。

  • By December I will have been teaching here for twenty years. 到十二月，我在这里教书就满二十年了。

13. 到 16：过去将来

这些时态站在过去看后来的事，最常见于宾语从句。主句是 said、told、knew、thought 这类过去动词。

  • 过去将来：He said he would call me. 他说他会给我打电话。
  • 过去将来进行：He said he would be waiting at the gate. 他说他会在门口等。
  • 过去将来完成：She hoped she would have finished the paper before the deadline. 她希望截止日期前能写完论文。
  • 过去将来完成进行：He told me that by 2020 he would have been living abroad for ten years. 他告诉我，到 2020 年他在国外就住满十年了。

was / were going to 也可以表示过去看来将要发生的事：I knew she was going to leave. 我知道她要走。

时态选择的四个问题

拿不准用哪个时态时，按顺序问：

  1. 有没有明确的过去时间，如 yesterday、in 2019、ago？有，优先一般过去时。
  2. 有没有 since、for、already、yet、ever，而且和现在有关？有，优先现在完成时。
  3. 是不是“过去的过去”？先发生的用过去完成时。
  4. 是不是强调某一时刻正在做？用进行时。

九、被动语态

主动句强调谁做的，被动句强调事情发生在谁身上。执行者不知道、不重要，或者不想说时，用被动。

公式：be + 过去分词。时态只体现在 be 上，过去分词不变。

改写四步，用 The chef cooked the meal. 做例子。

  1. 把主动句的宾语 the meal 拿到主语位置。
  2. 按原来的时态写出 be。cooked 是过去时，所以 be 用 was。
  3. 动词改成过去分词 cooked。
  4. 原来的主语用 by 放到后面。不重要就可以省。

得到：The meal was cooked by the chef. 这顿饭是厨师做的。

各时态的被动，以 repair the road 为例：

  • 一般现在：The road is repaired every year. 这条路每年修。
  • 一般过去：The road was repaired last year.
  • 现在进行：The road is being repaired. 路正在修。
  • 过去进行：The road was being repaired when I passed.
  • 现在完成：The road has been repaired.
  • 过去完成：The road had been repaired before winter.
  • 一般将来：The road will be repaired next month.
  • 过去将来：He said the road would be repaired soon.
  • 情态动词：The road must be repaired. The road can be repaired today.

否定把 not 放在第一个助动词后面：The road is not repaired. The road has not been repaired. The road must not be closed.

双宾语有两种改法。She gave me a book.

  • 人作主语：I was given a book.
  • 物作主语：A book was given to me.

宾补留在原处，变成主语补足语。

  • We elected her captain. → She was elected captain. 她当选为队长。

短语动词的小品词一个都不能丢。

  • The children are looked after well. 孩子们被照顾得很好。
  • 别写成 are looked well。after 必须留下。

get + 过去分词多用于突然的、不如意的事。

  • He got hurt in the accident. 他在事故中受了伤。
  • She got paid yesterday. 她昨天领到了工资。

没有被动的情况

不及物动词没有宾语，也就没有被动。

  • The accident happened yesterday. 别写成 was happened。
  • Great changes have taken place. 别写成 have been taken place。

系动词 look、seem、become、feel 后面接形容词，不用被动。

  • The cloth feels soft. 这块布摸起来很软。别写成 is felt soft。

有些动词用主动形式表示被动意思：

  • The book sells well. 这本书卖得好。
  • The door won’t open. 这门打不开。
  • The pen writes smoothly. 这支笔写起来很顺。
  • The dish tastes good. 这道菜味道好。

十、情态动词

情态动词表示能力、许可、义务、推测。它后面接动词原形，自己没有人称变化，不能再加 s。否定和疑问都由情态动词完成，不需要 do。

  • She can swim. 别写成 She can swims. 也别写成 She cans swim.
  • Can she swim? / She cannot swim. 缩写 can’t。

1. 能力、许可、必须

can / could

  • 能力：She can swim. 她会游泳。
  • 过去的能力：She could swim at the age of five. 她五岁就会游泳。
  • 请求，could 更客气：Could you open the door? 你能开一下门吗？
  • can 也可以表示许可：You can go now. 你现在可以走了。

be able to 可以用于各种时态，can 本身没有将来式和完成式。

  • I will be able to drive next year. 我明年就会开车了。
  • He was able to escape. 他设法逃出来了。（过去做成了某一件具体的事，用 was able to，不只是有能力）

may / might

  • 许可：May I come in? 我可以进来吗？
  • 可能，might 更没把握：It may rain. It might rain later. 也许会下雨。

must 和 have to

  • must 是说话人认为“必须”：You must wear a seat belt. 你必须系安全带。
  • have to 是客观情况迫使：I have to leave now. The bus is coming. 车来了，我得走了。
  • must 的过去和将来用 had to、will have to：I had to walk home. 我只好走回家。

must 的两个否定意思完全不同：

  • mustn’t 表示禁止：You mustn’t smoke here. 不许在这里抽烟。
  • needn’t / don’t have to 表示不必：You needn’t come. 你不必来。

should / ought to 表示应该，ought to 语气稍重。

  • You should see a doctor. 你应该去看医生。
  • You ought to apologize. 你应该道歉。

shall

  • Shall I open the window? 我把窗户打开好吗？（征求意见）
  • Shall we begin? 我们开始吧？

will / would

  • 意愿：I will help you.
  • 请求，would 更客气：Would you like some coffee? Would you mind opening the window?

need 和 dare

两个词都能当情态动词，也能当实义动词。

  • 情态动词：Need I go? No, you needn’t. He dare not tell her.
  • 实义动词：Do I need to go? He doesn’t dare to tell her. 第三人称要加 s：He needs to go. She dares to try.

2. 对现在或将来的推测

肯定程度从高到低：must → should → may → might。否定的“不可能”用 can’t，不用 mustn’t。

  • The light is on. He must be at home. 灯亮着，他一定在家。
  • That can’t be true. 那不可能是真的。
  • She may be busy. 她也许很忙。
  • He might come later. 他也许晚点来。

mustn’t 是“不许”，不是“不可能”。他不可能在家，要说 He can’t be at home.

3. 对过去的推测和评价

公式是：情态动词 + have + 过去分词。

  • must have done：过去一定做了。He must have left early. 他一定早走了。地上的包已经不在了。
  • can’t / couldn’t have done：过去不可能。She can’t have forgotten. I reminded her twice. 她不可能忘了，我提醒过她两次。
  • may / might have done：过去也许。They might have missed the train. 他们也许没赶上火车。
  • should / ought to have done：本该做，实际没做。You should have told me. 你本该告诉我。
  • shouldn’t have done：本不该做，实际做了。You shouldn’t have shouted. 你本不该那么喊。
  • needn’t have done：本不必做，实际做了。You needn’t have waited. I had the key. 你本不必等，我有钥匙。
  • could have done：本来可以，实际没做。We could have taken a taxi. 我们本来可以打车。

didn’t need to do 和 needn’t have done 不一样。

  • I didn’t need to wait. 我当时不必等，所以我也没等。
  • I needn’t have waited. 我本不必等，可是我等了。

4. 三个 used to，不要混

  • used to do：过去常常做，现在不做了。I used to live in a small town. 我过去住在小镇上。
    • 否定：I didn’t use to live here. 或 I used not to live here.
    • 疑问：Did you use to live here?
  • be used to doing：习惯于。I am used to getting up early. 我习惯早起。to 是介词。
  • be used to do：被用来做。Wood is used to make paper. 木头被用来造纸。这是 use 的被动。

5. had better 和 would rather

  • had better do：最好做。You had better leave now.
  • 否定是 had better not do：You had better not be late. 别写成 had better not to do。
  • would rather do：宁可。I would rather stay home.
  • would rather do than do：宁可……也不……。I would rather walk than take a bus. 我宁可走路，也不坐公交。

十一、非谓语动词

一个英语简单句只能有一个谓语。如果还要再出现一个动词，这个动词就要变成不定式、动名词或分词，不再单独当谓语。

先用这个办法选形式：

  • 这个动词在句子里相当于一个名词吗？考虑 to do 或 doing。
  • 它表示目的、将来、某一次具体的事吗？多用 to do。
  • 它表示习惯，或者跟在 enjoy、finish 后面吗？用 doing。
  • 它修饰名词，和名词是主动、正在进行吗？用 doing。
  • 它修饰名词，和名词是被动、已经完成吗？用 done。

1. 不定式 to do

作主语

  • To learn a language takes time. 学一门语言需要时间。
  • 句子显得头重时，用 it 占着主语的位置：It takes time to learn a language.

作宾语

  • I want to leave. 我想离开。
  • 后面如果再接形容词补语，也用 it 作形式宾语：I find it hard to learn physics. 我发现物理很难学。
    • 别写成 I find to learn physics hard.

常见接 to do 的动词：want、hope、wish、decide、plan、agree、refuse、promise、expect、afford、manage、pretend、offer、learn。

作定语，放在名词后面，常表示将要做的事。

  • I have a meeting to attend. 我有个会要参加。
  • She is the first student to arrive. 她是第一个到的学生。
  • 如果不定式的动词是不及物，要保留介词：I need a pen to write with. 我需要一支用来写字的笔。He has no room to live in.

作状语，表示目的、结果或原因。

  • He stopped to rest. 他停下来，为的是休息。
  • He got up early to catch the bus. 他早起去赶公交。
  • I’m glad to see you. 见到你我很高兴。

作宾补

  • She asked me to wait. 她让我等。
  • 有些动词后面的不定式不带 to：let、make、have，以及 see、hear、watch、notice、feel。
    • Let me go. 让我走。
    • The boss made him work ten hours. 老板让他工作了十小时。
    • I saw him enter the room. 我看见他进了房间。（看见了全过程）
  • 这些句子变成被动后，要把 to 加回来：He was made to work ten hours. He was seen to enter the room.

疑问词 + to do 相当于一个名词。

  • I don’t know what to say. 我不知道该说什么。= I don’t know what I should say.
  • Tell me where to go. 告诉我该去哪儿。
  • whether 可以这样用，if 不行：I don’t know whether to leave.

to 后面省略动词：为了避免重复，只留下 to。

  • Would you like to come? Yes, I’d like to. 我愿意。
  • 别只说 I’d like.

2. 动名词 doing

动名词在句子里当名词用，表示一件事，常常是习惯或一般情况。

  • 主语：Swimming is good exercise. 游泳是很好的锻炼。
  • 宾语：She enjoys reading. 她喜欢阅读。
  • 表语：His job is teaching. 他的工作是教书。对比：His job is to teach these children. 不定式更强调具体的一次任务。
  • 定语：a swimming pool 游泳池。这里的 swimming 说明池子的用途，不是“正在游泳”。

只接 doing、不接 to do 的动词，可以记这几个：enjoy、finish、mind、avoid、practice、suggest、keep、consider、admit、deny、risk、imagine、miss、delay。

  • Would you mind opening the window? 你介意开一下窗吗？
  • He avoided answering the question. 他回避了这个问题。
  • He kept asking the same question. 他不停地问同一个问题。

介词后面的动词也要用 doing。

  • Thank you for helping me.
  • He left without saying goodbye.
  • I look forward to seeing you. to 在这里是介词。
  • She is used to getting up early.

to do 和 doing 意思不同的动词，用“时间”来区分：to do 多指还没做，doing 多指已经做。

  • remember to do 记得要做：Remember to lock the door. 出门前记得锁门。
  • remember doing 记得做过：I remember locking the door. 我记得锁过了。
  • forget to do 忘了要做：I forgot to lock the door. 我忘了锁门。
  • forget doing 忘了做过的事：I forgot meeting him. 我忘了曾经见过他。
  • stop to do 停下来，改去做另一件事：He stopped to smoke. 他停下来去抽烟。
  • stop doing 停止正在做的事：He stopped smoking. 他戒烟了。
  • try to do 努力、试图：Try to finish it today. 尽量今天做完。
  • try doing 试一试这个办法：Try restarting the computer. 试试重启电脑。
  • mean to do 打算：I didn’t mean to hurt you. 我不是有意伤害你。
  • mean doing 意味着：This means waiting another hour. 这意味着还要再等一小时。
  • regret to do 遗憾地通知：I regret to tell you that the flight is canceled.
  • regret doing 后悔做过：I regret telling him the truth.
  • go on to do 接着做另一件事：After reading the text, he went on to do the exercises.
  • go on doing 继续同一件事：He went on reading. 他继续读。

need、want、require 后面接 doing，常用主动形式表示被动意思。

  • The bike needs repairing. = The bike needs to be repaired. 自行车需要修。

3. 分词

现在分词 doing：主动、正在进行。过去分词 done：被动、已经完成。

修饰名词：

  • the sleeping baby 正在睡觉的婴儿（婴儿自己在睡）
  • the broken window 破碎的窗户（窗户被打破）
  • the falling leaves 正在落的叶子
  • the fallen leaves 已经落下的叶子
  • a boring film 令人厌烦的电影（主动：电影让人烦）
  • a bored student 感到厌烦的学生（被动：学生自己感到烦）

-ing 和 -ed 的情感形容词，修饰人多用 -ed，修饰物多用 -ing：excited people，exciting news；interested students，interesting books。

作宾补：

  • I saw him crossing the street. 我看见他正在过马路。（进行中）
  • I saw him cross the street. 我看见他过了马路。（全过程，用不带 to 的不定式）
  • I heard the song sung in French. 我听见这首歌被用法语演唱。（歌是被唱的，用过去分词）

作状语时，分词的逻辑主语必须是句子的主语。

  • Seeing the police, the man ran away. 那人看见警察就跑了。seeing 的主语是 the man。
  • Seen from the hill, the town looks small. 从小山上看，小镇显得很小。town 是被看的对象，所以用 seen。
  • 别写成 Seeing from the hill, the town looks small. 小镇自己不会看。

否定把 not 放在分词前面：Not knowing what to do, she asked for help. 她不知道该怎么办，就请人帮忙。

分词完成式 having done 表示这个动作在谓语之前已经完成。

  • Having finished the homework, he went out to play. 做完作业后，他出去玩了。

4. 独立主格

分词的主语和句子主语不是同一个人时，要在分词前写出它自己的主语。

  • Weather permitting, we will go hiking. 天气允许的话，我们就去远足。permitting 的主语是 weather，不是 we。
  • He stood there, his hands in his pockets. 他站在那里，双手插在口袋里。
  • The meeting over, everyone left. 会议结束，大家都走了。over 前面的 being 可以省略。
  • Time permitting 也可以写成 If time permits.

with 也可以带这种结构：

  • He left with the light on. 他走了，灯还开着。
  • She sat with her eyes closed. 她坐着，眼睛闭着。

十二、虚拟语气

虚拟语气说的不是事实。先把事实写出来，再按“时间往前推一格”来改。

假设的时间 if 从句 主句 事实
与现在相反 过去式（be 用 were） would / could / might + 原形 现在不是这样
与过去相反 had + 过去分词 would / could / might + have done 过去没这么做
与将来相反，可能性很小 过去式，或 should do，或 were to do would / could / might + 原形 将来大概不会发生

与现在相反

事实：我不是你。

  • If I were you, I would apologize. 如果我是你，我会道歉。
  • If he had time, he would help us. 如果他有时间，他会帮我们。事实是他现在没有时间。
  • 口语里 If I was you 能听到，书面和考试用 were。

与过去相反

事实：你没有早点出发，所以没赶上火车。

  • If you had left earlier, you would have caught the train.
  • If I had studied harder, I could have passed. 如果我当时更用功，我就通过了。

与将来相反或可能性极小

  • If it should rain tomorrow, we would cancel the picnic. 万一下雨，我们就取消野餐。
  • If I were to win, I would travel around the world. 要是我赢了，我就去环游世界。

真实的将来条件不用虚拟：If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. 这里下雨是有可能的，从句用一般现在，主句用 will。

从句和主句时间不一致，各管各的。

事实：你过去没听劝，所以你现在不安全。

  • If you had taken his advice, you would be safer now.
  • 从句是过去，用 had taken；主句是现在，用 would be。

省略 if：把 were、had、should 提前，去掉 if。

  • Were I you, I would apologize.
  • Had you left earlier, you would have caught the train.
  • Should it rain, we would cancel the picnic.

wish 和 if only

wish 后面的时态也往前推。

  • 与现在相反：I wish I knew the answer. 我希望我知道答案。事实是我不知道。
  • 与过去相反：I wish I had studied harder. 我真希望当时更用功。
  • 与将来相反，往往表示不大可能实现：I wish it would stop raining. 但愿雨会停。

if only 的时态和 wish 一样，语气更强，常单独成句。

  • If only I had listened! 要是我当时听了就好了！

as if / as though

  • He talks as if he knew everything. 他说话的样子好像什么都知道。事实是他并不知道，所以用 knew。
  • 如果看起来是真的，就用陈述语气：It looks as if it is going to rain. 看起来真的要下雨。

建议、命令、要求

suggest、advise、insist、order、demand、require、request、recommend、propose 后面的 that 从句用 (should) + 动词原形。should 可以不写出来，但动词仍然用原形。

  • The doctor suggested that she (should) rest. 医生建议她休息。
  • He ordered that the work (should) be finished today. 他命令今天必须完成工作。
  • It is necessary that everyone be on time. 每个人都必须准时。
  • It is important that he (should) attend the meeting.

insist 有两个意思。

  • 坚持要求去做，用 should + 原形：He insisted that she (should) go. 他坚持要她去。
  • 坚持说一件事实，用陈述语气：He insisted that he was innocent. 他坚持说自己是无辜的。

suggest 表示“表明、暗示”时，也用陈述语气：His smile suggested that he was happy. 他的微笑表明他很高兴。

另外三个句型

  • It is time (that) + 过去式：该做某事了。It is time we went home. = It is time for us to go home. 我们该回家了。
  • would rather 后面接另一个人做某事，用过去式表示现在或将来：I’d rather you came tomorrow. 我更希望你明天来。
  • 表示过去，用 had done：I’d rather you hadn’t told him. 我宁愿你当时没告诉他。
  • would rather 后面如果还是同一个人，就用动词原形：I’d rather stay home.

十三、介词

介词后面接名词、代词或动名词，不能接句子。I am interested in learning English. learning 是动名词。别写成 in learn，也别写成 in I learn。

1. 时间：at、on、in

可以按范围从小到大记。

  • at 点钟和较短的时刻：at 7 o’clock，at noon，at night，at the moment，at the age of 16
  • on 某一天，或某一天的上午、下午、晚上：on Monday，on May 1st，on Monday morning，on a cold evening，on my birthday
  • in 周、月、年、季节、世纪，以及一天里较长的部分：in a week 的“在一周内”要看上下文；in July，in 2024，in spring，in the morning，in the 21st century

其他时间介词：

  • for + 一段时间：for two hours，for three years
  • since + 时间点：since 2019，since Monday，since he left
  • during 在某段期间之内：during the holiday，during the meeting
  • by 不晚于某个时间：Finish it by Friday. 星期五之前完成。
  • until 直到：Wait until six. 等到六点。
  • not … until 直到……才：He didn’t leave until six. 他直到六点才离开。
  • in + 一段时间，表示从现在算起多久以后：I’ll be back in an hour. 我一小时后回来。
  • after + 时间点：after six，after school

2. 地点

  • at 较小的点：at the bus stop，at the door，at the top of the page
  • in 较大范围或在内部：in China，in the room，in the sky，in the east
  • on 在表面上，也用于街道：on the desk，on the wall，on Nanjing Road
  • 城市前一般用 in：in Beijing。如果把城市看成地图上的一个点，也可以说 at Beijing，但高中阶段到达城市用 arrive in Beijing。

上下和内外：

  • above 在上方，不接触：The lamp is above the table.
  • over 在正上方，或盖住：She put a cloth over the cake. A plane flew over the city.
  • under 在正下方：The cat is under the table.
  • below 在下方，不一定正下方：The temperature is below zero.
  • between 两者之间：The school is between the park and the river.
  • among 三者及以上之间：She sat among her friends.
  • across 从表面上横穿：walk across the street，swim across the river
  • through 从内部穿过：walk through the forest，go through the tunnel
  • past 从旁边经过：walk past the post office
  • along 沿着：walk along the river
  • across from 在……对面：The bank is across from the school.

in、on、to 表示方位时：

  • Shanghai is in the east of China. 上海在中国境内的东部。
  • Japan is to the east of China. 日本在中国境外的东边。
  • Mongolia is on the north of China. 蒙古和中国北部接壤。

3. 必须整块记的搭配

这些介词不能按中文一个字一个字对。

  • arrive in + 大地方，arrive at + 小地方：arrive in Beijing，arrive at the station
  • depend on：It depends on the weather.
  • listen to：Listen to me.
  • be good at：She is good at math.
  • be interested in：He is interested in history.
  • be famous for 因……出名：The town is famous for its tea.
  • be famous as 作为……出名：He is famous as a writer.
  • be proud of：We are proud of you.
  • be angry with somebody，be angry at something：She was angry with me. She was angry at the delay.
  • be different from：This is different from that.
  • be full of：The bottle is full of water.
  • be made of 看得出原材料：The desk is made of wood.
  • be made from 看不出原材料：Paper is made from wood.
  • be made in 产地：It was made in China.
  • be made by 制造者：The cake was made by my mother.
  • spend time on something，spend time (in) doing：He spent an hour on the problem. He spent an hour solving it.
  • prevent … from doing：The rain prevented us from going out.
  • thanks to 多亏：Thanks to his help, we finished on time.

十四、连词

连词把词和词、句子和句子接起来。并列连词连接的两边地位相同；从属连词引出从句。

1. 并列连词

  • and 和、并且、然后：She finished the work and went home.
  • but 但是：The room is small but bright.
  • or 或者；否则：Tea or coffee? Hurry up, or we will be late.
  • so 所以：It was late, so we left.
  • for 因为，补充说明，不能放在句首回答 why：He must be ill, for he is absent.
  • yet 然而：She is young, yet very experienced.
  • while 然而，表示对比：I like tea, while he likes coffee.

成对使用的连词，两边词性要一致。

  • both … and … 两者都：Both the teacher and the students were pleased. 动词用复数。
  • either … or … 要么……要么……：Either you or I am wrong. 动词跟靠近的主语 I。
  • neither … nor … 既不……也不……：Neither he nor his parents know the address.
  • not only … but also … 不但……而且……：Not only the students but also the teacher was moved. 动词跟 teacher。
  • not only 放在句首时，前半句要倒装：Not only did he finish the work, but he also helped others.

中文里的“因为……所以……”“虽然……但是……”在英语里只能留一个连词。

  • Because he was ill, he stayed home. 或者 He was ill, so he stayed home.
  • 别写成 Because he was ill, so he stayed home.
  • Although he is young, he is calm.
  • 别写成 Although he is young, but he is calm.

2. 简单句、并列句、复合句

  • 简单句只有一套主谓：The children are playing.
  • 并列句有两套主谓，中间是 and、but、or、so：I called him, but he didn’t answer.
  • 复合句有主句和从句：I know that he is right.

两个句子如果都有主谓，中间不能只用逗号连接。

  • 别写成 I called him, he didn’t answer.
  • 可以改成 I called him, but he didn’t answer. 或者 I called him. He didn’t answer.

十五、名词性从句

名词性从句在句子里充当名词，可以当主语、宾语、表语、同位语。

引导词怎么选：

  • 从句意思完整，不缺成分，用 that。that 没有词义。
  • 从句意思完整，但要表示“是否”，用 whether 或 if。
  • 从句缺人，用 who、whom、whose。
  • 从句缺物，用 what。
  • 从句缺时间、地点、原因、方式，用 when、where、why、how。

所有名词性从句都用陈述语序：引导词 + 主语 + 谓语。不要把助动词提前。

1. 宾语从句

  • I think (that) you are right. 我认为你是对的。that 可以省略。
  • I wonder whether she will come. 我想知道她会不会来。
  • Can you tell me where he lives? 你能告诉我他住在哪里吗？
    • 别写成 where does he live。从句里主语 he 要放在动词前面。

whether 和 if 的选择：

  • 两者都可以：I don’t know whether / if he will come.
  • 后面紧跟 or not，用 whether：I don’t know whether or not he will come.
  • 介词后面用 whether：It depends on whether he agrees.
  • discuss 后面用 whether：We discussed whether we should go.
  • 不定式前面用 whether：I don’t know whether to go.

时态：主句是过去时，从句一般也改成过去的某种时态。

  • He says he is tired. 主句是现在，从句可以是现在。
  • He said he was tired. 主句是过去，从句改成过去。
  • 从句是客观真理、格言时，保持一般现在时：The teacher said the earth goes around the sun.

否定转移：think、believe、suppose、expect 后面的宾语从句如果是否定意思，常常把 not 放到主句。

  • I don’t think he is right. 我认为他不对。
  • 反意疑问句跟着从句走：I don’t think he is right, is he?

it 可以作形式宾语，真正的宾语放到后面。

  • I think it necessary that we start early. 我认为我们有必要早点出发。
  • We made it clear that the plan had changed.

2. 主语从句

  • What he said surprised us. 他说的话让我们吃了一惊。what 在从句里作 said 的宾语。
  • That she passed is good news. 她通过了，这是好消息。that 不能省略，因为它在句首。
  • 更常见的是用 it 作形式主语：It is good news that she passed.
  • It is clear that we need more time. 显然我们需要更多时间。
  • It is unknown whether they will accept the plan. 还不知道他们是否接受这个计划。
  • It doesn’t matter who will go. 谁去都没关系。

3. 表语从句

放在系动词后面。

  • The question is whether we can finish on time. 问题是我们能否按时完成。
  • That is why he left. 那就是他离开的原因。
  • This is what I want. 这就是我想要的。
  • The reason is that he was ill. 原因是他病了。
    • reason 作主语时，表语从句用 that，不用 because。
    • 也可以说 He was absent because he was ill. 这是原因状语从句，不是表语从句。

4. 同位语从句

同位语从句说明前面那个抽象名词的具体内容。常见名词：fact、news、idea、hope、doubt、question、promise、suggestion、truth、message。

  • The news that our team won spread quickly. 我们队赢了，这个消息很快传开。
  • I have no idea when he will arrive. 我不知道他什么时候到。
  • The fact that he lied surprised everyone. 他撒谎了这一事实让所有人吃惊。

和定语从句的区别，就看 that 在从句里干不干活。

  • The news that he told me is true. 他告诉我的那个消息是真的。that 代替 news，作 told 的宾语，这是定语从句。
  • The news that he resigned is true. 他辞职了，这个消息是真的。that 在从句里不作成分，只是连接，这是同位语从句。

十六、定语从句

定语从句修饰一个名词或代词，把它放在那个词后面，翻译成“……的”。被修饰的词叫先行词。

选关系词用四步：

  1. 找出先行词，看它是人还是物，是时间、地点还是 reason。
  2. 把先行词代回从句，看它在从句里作主语、宾语、定语，还是作状语。
  3. 作主语或宾语，用人称 who / whom / that，用物用 which / that。作定语用 whose。
  4. 作时间、地点、原因状语，才用 when、where、why。如果从句里缺的是主语或宾语，即使先行词是时间或地点，也要用 that / which。

1. 关系代词

  • who 指人，作主语：The man who lives next door is a doctor. 住在隔壁的那个人是医生。who 在从句里作 lives 的主语。
  • whom 指人，作宾语，可以省略：The man (whom) you met is my uncle. 你遇见的那个人是我叔叔。
  • whose 表示“……的”，人或物都可以：
    • The girl whose bag was stolen called the police. 包被偷的那个女孩报了警。
    • He lives in a house whose windows face south. 他住在一所窗户朝南的房子里。whose windows = the windows of the house。
  • which 指物：The book which is on the desk is mine.
  • that 人和物都可以，只能用于限制性定语从句：The letter that arrived today is from my sister.

that 或 which 在从句里作宾语时可以省略。

  • The book (that) I bought is interesting. 我买的那本书很有意思。

介词可以留在从句末尾，也可以提前。提前之后只能用 which 或 whom，不能用 that，也不能省略。

  • This is the house which he was born in.
  • This is the house in which he was born. 这是他出生的那所房子。
  • in which 在这里也可以换成 where。
  • The person to whom you spoke is the manager. 和你说话的那个人是经理。

这些情况优先用 that：

  • 先行词是 all、everything、nothing、something、anything、little、much：Is there anything that I can do?
  • 先行词被最高级、序数词修饰：This is the best film that I have seen. He is the first student that arrived.
  • 先行词被 the only、the very、the same 修饰：He is the only person that can help.
  • 先行词既有人又有物：They talked about the people and things that they remembered.

这些情况不能用 that：

  • 逗号后面的非限制性定语从句：My father, who is over sixty, still works.
  • 介词提前：the house in which he was born

2. 关系副词

  • when 在从句里作时间状语：I remember the day when we first met. when = on which。
  • where 作地点状语：This is the town where I grew up. where = in which。
  • why 作原因状语，先行词通常是 reason：That is the reason why she refused. why = for which。

先行词是地点，但从句缺主语或宾语时，不用 where。

  • This is the town which / that I visited. visited 后面缺宾语，所以用 which 或 that，不用 where。
  • This is the town where I was born. was born 不缺宾语，缺的是“在那里”，所以用 where。

3. 限制性和非限制性

限制性定语从句用来确定是哪一个，不能去掉，前面没有逗号。

  • My brother who lives in Hangzhou is a lawyer. 我那个住在杭州的哥哥是律师。言下之意，我可能还有别的哥哥。

非限制性定语从句只是补充说明，前后有逗号，拿掉以后主句仍然清楚。不能用 that，关系词也不能省略。

  • My brother, who lives in Hangzhou, is a lawyer. 我哥哥是律师，他住在杭州。只有这一个哥哥。
  • He missed the train, which made him late. 他没赶上火车，这使他迟到了。which 指前面整句话，不是指 train。

as 也可以引导非限制性定语从句，位置比 which 灵活，常翻译成“正如”。

  • As we expected, the plan worked. 正如我们预料的那样，计划奏效了。
  • He was late, as is often the case. 他迟到了，这是常有的事。
  • as 可以放在句首，which 指整句时通常不放在句首。

the same … as，such … as 中的 as 也是关系词。

  • He is not the same person as he used to be. 他和从前不一样了。

十七、状语从句

状语从句修饰动词或整句。高中最重要的一条是：时间、条件、让步从句里，用一般现在时表示将来，不要用 will。

  • I will call you when I arrive. 我到了就给你打电话。
  • 别写成 when I will arrive。
  • If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.
  • 主句里的 will 保留。只有从句不用 will。

1. 时间

when、while、as、before、after、until、since、as soon as、once、every time、the moment。

  • When I got home, my mother was cooking. 我到家时，妈妈正在做饭。when 既可以接点时间，也可以接一段时间。
  • While I was reading, the light went out. while 后面多是延续的动作，强调“在那段时间里”。
  • As time went by, he became more confident. as 常表示“随着”，两个动作同时变化。
  • I had finished the work before he came. 他来之前我已经做完了。
  • I have lived here since I graduated. 我毕业以后一直住在这里。since 从句里用一般过去时，主句用现在完成时。
  • As soon as he arrived, the meeting began. 他一到，会议就开始了。
  • Once you begin, you must go on. 一旦开始，你就必须继续。
  • I recognized him the moment I saw him. 我一看见他就认出来了。

until 的两种意思：

  • 主句是延续性动词，表示“一直到”：Wait until six. I waited until he came. 我一直等到他来。
  • 主句是否定的短暂动词，表示“直到……才”：He didn’t leave until six. He didn’t go to bed until he finished his homework.

2. 地点

  • Put it where you can see it. 把它放在你看得见的地方。
  • Where there is a will, there is a way. 有志者事竟成。
  • Wherever you go, I will follow. 无论你去哪里，我都跟着。

3. 原因

  • because 语气最强，用来回答 why：He stayed home because he was ill.
  • since 表示双方都知道的原因，常常放在句首：Since everyone is here, let’s start. 既然大家都到了，我们开始吧。
  • as 语气较弱，附带说明原因：As it was raining, we took a taxi.
  • now that 既然，和 since 接近：Now that you are free, you can help me.

because、since、as 引导从句；so 连接并列句。一句里不要同时用 because 和 so。

4. 目的

so that、in order that 后面常常有 can、could、may、might、will、would。

  • She spoke loudly so that everyone could hear. 她大声说，好让每个人都听见。
  • He got up early in order that he would not miss the bus. 他早起，以免错过公交。

和不定式比较：主语一致时，可以用 in order to、so as to。

  • He got up early in order to catch the bus.
  • so as to 一般不放在句首。In order to catch the bus, he got up early. 可以。

否定目的：

  • Take an umbrella in case it rains. 带把伞，以防下雨。in case 从句里常用一般现在时。
  • She closed the window for fear that it would rain. 她关上窗，唯恐下雨。

5. 结果

  • so + 形容词/副词 + that：The box was so heavy that I couldn’t lift it. 箱子太重，我抬不起来。
  • such + a/an + 形容词 + 单数名词 + that：It was such a heavy box that I couldn’t lift it.
  • such + 形容词 + 复数名词/不可数名词 + that：They are such good students that every teacher likes them.
  • so + many / much / few / little + 名词 + that：There were so many people that we couldn’t get in.

so that 既可以表目的，也可以表结果。看从句里有没有 can、could，以及有没有逗号。

  • He got up early so that he could catch the bus. 目的。
  • He got up late, so that he missed the bus. 结果：他起晚了，结果没赶上车。

6. 条件

  • if：If you heat ice, it melts. 给冰加热，冰就会化。这是客观事实，主从句都可以用一般现在时。
  • 对将来的真实条件：If it rains, we will cancel the game. 从句现在时，主句将来时。
  • unless = if … not：You will fail unless you work harder. = You will fail if you don’t work harder.
  • as long as / so long as 只要：You may leave as long as you finish the work.
  • on condition that 在……条件下：I will go on condition that you go with me.

与事实相反的条件用虚拟语气，见第十二节。不要把真实条件和虚拟条件写成同一种时态。

7. 让步

although、though、even though、even if 都表示“虽然、即使”。

  • Although it was cold, he went swimming. 虽然冷，他还是去游泳了。
  • Although 不和 but 连用，可以和 yet、still 连用：Although he is young, he is still very calm.
  • even if 更强调“即使如此，也不改变”：Even if I fail, I will try again. 即使失败，我也会再试。
  • while 也可以表示“虽然”，多放在句首：While I understand your idea, I don’t agree. 虽然我理解你的想法，但我不同意。

no matter + 疑问词 = 疑问词 + ever。

  • Whatever you say, I won’t change my mind. = No matter what you say, I won’t change my mind.
  • Wherever you go, I’ll be with you.
  • However hard the problem is, we can solve it. 无论这道题多难，我们都能解。however 后面紧跟形容词或副词。

as 和 though 可以倒装，语气更强。把表语、副词或动词原形提前。

  • Young as he is, he is calm. = Although he is young, he is calm.
  • 名词提前时不加冠词：Child as he is, he knows a lot. 别写成 A child as he is.
  • Try as she might, she couldn’t open the jar. 她怎么试也打不开那个罐子。

8. 方式和比较

  • Do as I told you. 按我告诉你的去做。
  • He looked as if he had seen a ghost. 他看起来好像看见了鬼。与事实相反，用虚拟。
  • This task is harder than I expected. 这个任务比我预想的难。
  • She works as carefully as her sister does. 她工作和她姐姐一样仔细。does 代替 works，避免重复。

十八、主谓一致

谓语用 is 还是 are，先找真正的主语，不要被主语和谓语之间的修饰语骗了。

1. 看形式

  • The book is new. The books are new.
  • 动名词、不定式、主语从句作主语，动词用单数。
    • Reading is a habit.
    • To see is to believe.
    • What he said is true.
  • and 连接两个不同的事物，动词用复数：Tom and Jerry are friends.
  • and 连接的两个名词指同一个人或同一件事，动词用单数：The singer and dancer is coming. 这位歌手兼舞者要来了。前面只有一个 the。
    • The singer and the dancer are coming. 歌手和舞者两个人要来。有两个 the。
  • every、each、no 修饰并列主语，动词用单数：Every boy and every girl has a chance.

2. 看意思

  • 集体名词 family、class、team、group、crowd 看成一个整体，用单数；看成成员，用复数。
    • The family is large. 这家人人丁多。
    • The family are watching TV. 这家人正在看电视。
  • people、police、cattle 本身就是复数：The police are on the way. 别写成 The police is.
  • 时间、距离、金钱、重量作为一个整体，用单数：Ten years is a long time. Five dollars is enough.
  • the number of + 名词，动词用单数，表示“……的数目”：The number of students is 40.
  • a number of + 复数名词，动词用复数，表示“许多”：A number of students are absent.
  • a pair of glasses 用单数：A pair of glasses is on the desk. 眼镜本身用复数时：My glasses are broken.

3. 看靠近动词的那个主语

either … or、neither … nor、not only … but also、there be，动词跟最近的主语。

  • Either the teacher or the students are wrong.
  • Either the students or the teacher is wrong.
  • There is a book and two pens on the desk.
  • There are two pens and a book on the desk.

这些词不算并列主语，动词仍然看前面的主语：together with、as well as、with、except、besides、including、rather than。

  • The teacher, together with the students, is in the lab. 老师和学生们在实验室。主语是 teacher。
  • Nobody except two girls was late.

十九、特殊句式

1. 疑问句

一般疑问句把 be、助动词或情态动词提前。没有这些词，就加 do / does / did。

  • Are you ready?
  • Can she drive?
  • Do you like tea?
  • Did he leave?

特殊疑问句 = 疑问词 + 一般疑问句的语序。

  • Where do you live?
  • Why did he leave?
  • How long have you lived here?

疑问词自己就是主语时，不要再倒装。

  • Who broke the cup? 谁打碎了杯子？who 是主语。
  • What happened? 发生了什么？
  • 对比：Who did you meet? you 才是主语，所以 did 要提前。

选择疑问句给出两个或几个选择，不能用 yes 或 no 回答。

  • Do you want tea or coffee? 回答 I’d like tea.

反意疑问句：前肯后否，前否后肯。后面的代词和前面的主语一致，时态也一致。

  • You are a student, aren’t you?
  • He doesn’t smoke, does he?
  • They have finished, haven’t they?
  • There is a bank nearby, isn’t there?

几个特殊情况：

  • I am late, aren’t I? 不用 amn’t I。
  • 祈使句后面多用 will you：Open the door, will you? Don’t be late, will you?
  • Let’s 包括对方，用 shall we：Let’s go, shall we?
  • Let us 请求对方允许，用 will you：Let us go, will you?
  • 陈述部分有 never、seldom、hardly、little、few、nobody、nothing，就算否定，后面用肯定：He seldom comes, does he?
  • 宾语从句的反意疑问通常跟主句：He said she was ill, didn’t he?
  • think、believe 的否定转移，跟从句：I don’t think she is ill, is she?

回答反意疑问句时，看事实，不看中文的“对”或“不对”。

  • He isn’t a teacher, is he? 事实是他真的不是老师：No, he isn’t. 中文说“对，他不是”。
  • 事实是他是老师：Yes, he is. 中文说“不，他是”。

2. There be

there be 表示“某地存在某物”，不是“某地有”就一定用 have。have 表示所属。

  • There is a bank near here. 这附近有一家银行。
  • I have a bike. 我有一辆自行车。这辆车是我的。

be 的单复数看后面第一个主语，时态按需要变化。

  • There is a book on the desk.
  • There are some books on the desk.
  • There was a cinema here ten years ago.
  • There will be a meeting tomorrow.
  • There used to be a tree in front of the house. 房子前面以前有一棵树。
  • There seems to be a problem. 似乎有个问题。
  • There happened to be a doctor nearby. 附近恰好有一位医生。

3. 倒装

倒装就是把平时放在主语后面的词挪到主语前面。先写正常语序，再移动，不容易错。

完全倒装：整个谓语放到主语前面。多用于地点副词或方位短语开头，而且主语是名词。

  • 正常：The bus comes here. → Here comes the bus. 车来了。
  • 正常：An old tree stood in front of the house. → In front of the house stood an old tree.
  • 主语是代词时不倒装：Here you are. 给你。Away they went. 他们走了。
  • There goes the bell. 铃响了。

部分倒装：只把助动词、be 或情态动词提前。句子里没有助动词，就补 do / does / did。

否定词放在句首：never、seldom、hardly、rarely、little、not until、by no means、no sooner … than、hardly … when。

  • 正常：I have never seen such a view. → Never have I seen such a view. 我从没见过这样的景色。
  • He seldom goes out. → Seldom does he go out. 句子原来没有助动词，所以补 does，goes 回到原形。
  • He didn’t come back until midnight. → Not until midnight did he come back. 直到半夜他才回来。
  • She had no sooner sat down than the phone rang. → No sooner had she sat down than the phone rang. 她刚坐下，电话就响了。
  • Hardly had I arrived when it began to rain. 我刚到就开始下雨了。

only + 状语放在句首，才倒装。only 修饰主语时不倒装。

  • Only then did I understand. 直到那时我才明白。
  • Only in this way can we succeed. 只有这样我们才能成功。
  • Only he knows the password. 只有他知道密码。he 是主语，不倒装。

so、neither、nor 表示“也”或“也不”。

  • She likes jazz, and so do I. 她喜欢爵士，我也喜欢。
  • He can’t swim, neither can I. 他不会游泳，我也不会。nor can I 也可以。
  • 如果是赞同同一件事，不用倒装：It is cold. So it is. 天很冷。的确冷。这里的 it 和前面是同一个主语。

so … that 里的 so 放到句首，主句倒装，that 从句不倒装。

  • He was so tired that he fell asleep at once. → So tired was he that he fell asleep at once.

as 引导的让步状语从句，把表语提前，见第十七节。

4. 强调

It is / was + 被强调的部分 + that / who + 其余部分。

原句：Tom broke the window yesterday.

  • 强调人：It was Tom who / that broke the window. 是汤姆打破了窗户。
  • 强调时间：It was yesterday that Tom broke the window. 汤姆是昨天打破窗户的。
  • 强调状语：It was in this room that we signed the paper. 我们是在这个房间里签的文件。

判断是不是强调句：去掉 It is / was … that，剩下的词还能组成完整的句子，就是强调句。

  • It was yesterday that I met her. 去掉后是 I met her yesterday. 完整，所以是强调句。
  • It is a problem that we all know. 去掉后是 a problem we all know，缺少谓语，这是“它是一个我们都知道的问题”，that 引导定语从句，不是强调句。

not … until 的强调：

  • He didn’t leave until six. → It was not until six that he left. 直到六点他才离开。
  • 强调句里 until 从句不再用否定，not 已经放在 until 前面。

强调谓语用 do / does / did + 动词原形，翻译成“的确、确实”。

  • I do want to help. 我确实想帮忙。
  • He did call you. 他确实给你打过电话。过去时的 did 一旦出现，call 用原形，不要写成 did called。

5. 省略

并列句里重复的成分可以省。

  • She can speak French, and he can (speak French) too.

状语从句里，如果从句主语和主句主语相同，而且从句动词是 be，主语和 be 可以一起省。

  • When (he was) asked about it, he kept silent. 被问到这件事时，他保持沉默。
  • While (you are) reading, don’t listen to music.
  • If (it is) necessary, I will stay. 如果有必要，我会留下。
  • Although (he was) tired, he went on working.

比较从句里重复的动词也可以省：She is taller than I (am). He runs faster than his brother (does).

二十、直接引语和间接引语

直接引语把原话用引号照抄下来。间接引语用自己的话转述，时态、人称、时间、地点通常都要改。

原句：He said, “I am busy today.”

转述：He said (that) he was busy that day. 他说他那天很忙。

改写步骤

  1. 去掉引号，必要时加上 that。
  2. 人称跟着转述者改。原话的 I 如果指说话人自己，就改成 he 或 she。
  3. 主句是过去时，从句的时态往过去推一档。
  4. 时间状语和地点状语跟着改。

时态对照：

  • am / is → was
  • are → were
  • do / does → did
  • did → had done
  • have / has done → had done
  • will → would
  • can → could
  • may → might
  • must → had to，表示义务时；表示推测可以不变

时间和地点：

  • now → then
  • today → that day
  • tonight → that night
  • yesterday → the day before
  • tomorrow → the next day / the following day
  • last week → the week before
  • next year → the next year
  • ago → before
  • here → there
  • this → that
  • these → those

再看一个完整的例子。

  • 直接：Tom said, “I will come here again tomorrow.”
  • 间接：Tom said that he would go there again the next day.
  • come here 改成 go there，是因为转述的人已经不在原来那个地方。如果转述时还在同一个地方，here 可以不改。

疑问句

语序改回陈述句。有疑问词就保留疑问词；没有疑问词就用 if 或 whether。

  • “Where do you live?” she asked. → She asked where I lived.
  • “Are you ready?” he asked. → He asked if I was ready. 也可以用 whether。
  • 问号改成句号。

祈使句

改成 ask / tell / order somebody to do。否定用 not to do。

  • “Please sit down,” she said. → She asked me to sit down.
  • “Don’t be late,” he said. → He told me not to be late.
  • “Go out!” the officer ordered. → The officer ordered the soldiers to go out.

可以不改时态的情况

转述的内容现在仍然是事实，或者是真理、习惯。

  • She said the museum opens at nine. 她说博物馆九点开门。现在仍然九点开。
  • He said he gets up at six every day. 如果这个习惯还在，可以不改。

二十一、把容易混的点放在一起

学完单项以后，用这几组对比检查自己有没有真正分开。

since 和 for

  • since Monday，since 2019，since he left：后面是起点。
  • for three days，for eight years：后面是一段长度。
  • He has lived here since 2019. He has lived here for many years.

few 和 a few，little 和 a little

  • a few friends 有几个朋友，肯定。
  • few friends 几乎没有朋友，否定。
  • a little water 有一点水。
  • little water 几乎没有水。

another 和 the other

  • Give me another cup. 再来一杯，没有限定只有两杯。
  • I have two pens. One is blue, and the other is black. 总共两个。

比较的两边

  • The weather in Kunming is better than that in Harbin.
  • The students in our class are more active than those in theirs.

主将从现

  • I will tell him when he comes.
  • If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the trip.
  • Even if it rains, I will go.

感官动词后面三种形式

  • I heard someone singing. 听见正在唱。
  • I heard someone sing a song. 听见唱完了一首歌。
  • I heard the song sung in French. 听见歌被用法语唱出来。

make 的主动和被动

  • They made him wait.
  • He was made to wait.

hope 和 suggest

  • I hope to see you. I hope that you will come.
  • 不能说 hope you to come。
  • She suggested going by train. She suggested that we (should) go by train.
  • 不能说 suggest to go。

否定的位置

  • I don’t think he is right. 否定在 think 前面，意思是否定后面的从句。
  • Not all birds can fly. 并非所有的鸟都会飞。部分否定。
  • None of the answers is correct. 全部否定。

to 是不定式还是介词

  • want to do，decide to do：to 是不定式符号，后面用动词原形。
  • look forward to doing，be used to doing，pay attention to doing，devote … to doing：to 是介词，后面用名词或动名词。

学语法时，每次只练一种变化。比如先把 I finished the work yesterday. 改成否定句和疑问句，再改成被动句 The work was finished yesterday. 再放进宾语从句 He said that the work had been finished. 一句话能这样来回改，这个语法点就算学会了。
